                                        Eye comfort

                                        These tools change how a webpage looks on your screen. They cannot change your monitor's real brightness, and they cannot promise to prevent eye strain.

                                        Checking the current page…

                                        Warm screen filter
                                        30%

                                        Applies to the current tab only. It does not block clicks, typing or scrolling. It is removed when the page reloads or you navigate away, and it does not tint full-screen video.

                                        Reading mode

                                        Sets a soft page background and dark-brown text. Pages that already use a dark background are skipped. Images, forms and links are not restyled.

                                        Screen-break reminders

                                        Reminders follow the interval you choose. FocusFlow does not measure your actual screen time. When one is due, a BREAK badge appears on the toolbar icon, and the message shows when you open FocusFlow. Reminders only run while Chrome is open.

                                        Settings

                                        Development mode is ON, so the durations below are ignored. Set DEV_MODE to false in js/config.js.

                                        Translation (English to Hindi)

                                        Right-click selected text and choose "Translate to Hindi". The selected text is sent to MyMemory, a third-party service (api.mymemory.translated.net). The free service has a daily limit and accepts up to 500 bytes per request. FocusFlow does not store or log your text.

                                        Optional. Turning it on asks Chrome for access to all websites, because the button must be able to see your text selection on any page. It never reads anything else, never appears over form fields, and sends text only when you click it (or press Alt+Shift+H). Turning it off removes that access.

                                        Reset all data

                                        Permanently deletes every task, note, timer state, statistic and setting stored by FocusFlow on this device.
